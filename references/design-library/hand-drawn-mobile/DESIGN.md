# Hand-drawn mobile

Use illustration to explain or welcome. The supplied hand-drawn reference pairs uneven ink outlines with heavy plain type, sparse cobalt annotations and pale paper.

- **Color.** Pale pink paper `#FBE6EB`, ink `#211A24` and cobalt `#2832A0`. Give the accent to a meaningful path or selection. Lay instructions out in ink.
- **Type.** Try Barlow Condensed ExtraBold with Atkinson Hyperlegible or system sans. Reserve hand-lettering for the illustration. Use the readable family for buttons, readings and instructions.
- **Marks.** Draw a few pressure-varied lines around one subject. Leave room for meaningful content and generous figure/ground contrast. Use a second family of marks only when it encodes something, such as ticks for levels.
- **Controls and motion.** Put a clean, large hit region behind the sketched contour. Nudge a relevant mark or draw a short line when the adjacent action changes. Keep the final shape visible with reduced motion.
