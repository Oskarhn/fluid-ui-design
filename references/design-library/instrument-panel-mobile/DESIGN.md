# Instrument panel on mobile

Use a physical visual language for a tuner, camera, audio unit or material tool. The supplied skeuomorphic reference shows brushed and dark materials, recesses, keys with physical travel and small lit indicators.

- **Color.** Graphite `#171C24`, raised surface `#2B3541`, light ink `#EEF5FA` and an amber indicator `#FFC072`. Use one direction of light for all parts and distinguish a shallow highlight from the lower edge.
- **Type.** Use a neutral sans for verbs and a mono face with tabular figures for measurements. Keep value and unit in place while the wheel moves.
- **Wheel.** Use a fixed track, turning center, active indicator and explicit finite sweep. Give the adjustable value a tap alternative and keyboard access. In SwiftUI use a native adjustable control or implement the equivalent accessibility actions.
- **Keys.** Design rest, press and selected states. Keep the tactile travel short. Add a text or shape change to the lit selected state.
- **Check.** Re-grab, sweep across the angular seam and clamp at both ends. The readable value survives reduced motion and a failed material effect.
