# Motion portfolio

Use work that can speak for itself. Select finished case studies in advance and obtain their approved posters, clips and credits.

- **Composition** — a top line states the studio's discipline, followed by a first project at 60–75% of the viewport width and a compact index along one edge. Separate clients with white space and clear names. On mobile put the project summary above its clip and place the next project within reach.
- **Look** — take the supporting neutral from the work. Use a 56–96px display face, 15–17px descriptions and 12–14px credits. Let the project artwork set the energy while keeping navigation consistently legible.
- **Motion** — load a poster as the default. Play one active preview on explicit hover/focus or with a visible play button, depending on the clip's role. Pause when the project leaves the viewport. Use a short opacity transition when moving between posters.
- **Fallback** — use correctly cropped stills where playback is unavailable or reduced motion is enabled. Preserve the project title and a direct link to its case study.
- **Check** — verify poster/first-frame matching, one active playback, orientation changes, and that a designer's credit stays associated with the right project.

Study input: [Haikoo Studio's project index and interaction hints](https://haikoo.org/), discovered through [SEESAW](https://www.seesaw.website/websites/haikoo).
