## A fixed opening and a moving landscape

The public gallery included a cyan-blue landscape seen through a dark window aperture. Keep the aperture fixed. Put two or three newly drawn horizon bands behind it and move distant bands less than nearby ones. Use a pale crop on the far side and a deep quiet edge under copy. Keep the foreground frame crisp at the portable width. [MotionSites backgrounds](https://motionsites.ai/backgrounds).

- **Build.** Draw periodic bands with low spatial frequencies. Move each band by a whole number of cycles over an eight-second loop. A single vertical mullion gives the eye a fixed comparison point.
- **Fallback.** Freeze at a frame with the bands separated and the aperture recognisable. Maintain focal framing in a narrow crop.

## Window light

Several watermarked cards put a high-exposure window or shaft of light at the edge of a dark interior. The still shows a useful brightness hierarchy. Source motion can vary from card to card. [MotionSites backgrounds](https://motionsites.ai/backgrounds).

- **Build.** Keep the aperture position fixed. Widen translucent wedges toward the floor and vary them by a few degrees. Place seeded dust in the lit half, using different phase offsets and bounded opacity. Leave the headline area visually stable.
- **Fallback.** Keep a single warm highlight and one defined window frame or shaft.

## Water reflection

A visible watermarked card uses a dark mountain/lake horizon and a small warm focal area with light reflected below. [MotionSites backgrounds](https://motionsites.ai/backgrounds).

- **Build.** Separate sky, silhouette and water with a clearly fixed horizon. Keep the light near one edge and break the reflected light into narrow strips that shift by phase. Increase spread nearer the viewer. Avoid moving the whole lake or duplicating the entire sky mechanically.
- **Fallback.** Export a frame that shows both the source and its reflection. Match the crop with a consistent object position.

## Layered arches

Visible cards include an arched garden opening and a repeated colonnade. Their nested shadows give depth, and the deepest opening carries the strongest contrast. [MotionSites backgrounds](https://motionsites.ai/backgrounds).

- **Build.** Draw new arch geometry at several depths, with different radii and three or more subdued lightness steps. Choose a single vanishing area and move layers at different distances around it. Render the lateral shift from one small periodic value. Use the deepest opening as the visual anchor.
- **Fallback.** Use one carefully composed still and retain space for foreground copy. On phones take a new crop or use the calmer color surface.
